# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want zig-js to be a project where anyone can contribute — regardless of
experience level, background, identity, or how they found their way here.
Everyone participating in this project's spaces is expected to help keep it that
way.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about code and kind about people. This project is technically
  demanding and holds a high evidence bar; that standard applies to work, never
  to the person doing it.
- Critique claims, not contributors. "This number has no source behind it" is
  the right conversation; "you don't know what you're doing" is not.
- Assume good faith. Most disagreements here are about missing context, and
  asking is cheaper than assuming.
- Accept that "no" is sometimes the answer, and that it usually comes with a
  reason. Ask for the reason if it is not obvious.
- Respect people's stated names and pronouns.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form.
- Personal attacks, demeaning comments, or sustained disruption of discussion.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately introducing insecure or malicious code, or misrepresenting
  evidence to get a change accepted.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests,
[Discussions](https://github.com/zig-utils/zig-js/discussions), and the
[Discord server](https://discord.gg/f7wBym6JF2) — and when representing the
project publicly.

## Reporting

Report a concern to **chrisbreuer93@gmail.com**. Reports are handled
confidentially, and you will get a response. If your report concerns the person
you would normally report to, say so and it will be routed elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this document, and may
warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban a participant. Enforcement aims to
be proportionate: a first, unintentional misstep should end with a private note
and a correction, not a ban.
